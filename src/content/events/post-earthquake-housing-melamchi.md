## Summary

Researchers presented findings from eighteen months of fieldwork in Melamchi following the 2024 flood-triggered displacement, focused on how reconstruction financing and community decision-making interacted — and where they broke down.

## Key takeaways

- Phased funding releases slowed reconstruction more than material shortages did.
- Households that led their own rebuilding process reported higher satisfaction, even when it took longer.
- Local government coordination was the single biggest predictor of timeline overruns.
