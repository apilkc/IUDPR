## What we'll cover

Participants will walk a route through the square with a conservation architect, stopping at structures that illustrate common risks — moisture damage, unsympathetic repairs, load issues — before a short workshop on photogrammetry and condition-survey basics using just a phone camera.

## Bring

Comfortable walking shoes, a smartphone, and a notebook. No prior heritage or architecture background required.
