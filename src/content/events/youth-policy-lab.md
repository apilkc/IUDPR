## Why this matters

Public space planning rarely includes the people who use it most. This session puts youth participants directly into the design process, working in small groups with IUDPR researchers to turn ideas into sketches a local government could actually act on.

## Format

In-person groups work at the IUDPR office; remote participants join breakout rooms over video and contribute to the same shared design boards.
