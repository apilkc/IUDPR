## Overview

Secondary cities face a different financing landscape than Kathmandu — smaller budgets, thinner private markets, and less access to international capital. This webinar walks through financing models that have worked elsewhere in South Asia and discusses what's realistically transferable.

## Who should attend

Municipal planners, cooperative housing organizers, and anyone working on housing policy outside the capital.
